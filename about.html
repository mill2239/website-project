About Coastal Crumbs Bakery

Learn about our story, style, and values.

Coastal Crumbs Bakery was created to bring together creativity, comfort, and a strong customer experience. Our bakery is inspired by soft coastal colors, warm service, and desserts that feel special but still approachable.

We focus on fresh ingredients, friendly service, and creative seasonal menu ideas. Whether customers stop in for a muffin or order a custom cake, our goal is to make every visit feel welcoming.
